Hello! This is one of my first projects in matlab, so it is likely rough around the edges.

What is it? - A tool to quickly plot and compare OpenRocket sim data and the real flight.

What are the goals? - To have a comprehensive tool to analyze every aspect of a flight easily

Currently Supported Altimeters:

Blue Raven 2 (LR .csv data)

ORK variables to export:

Time (s);

Altitude (ft);

Vertical velocity (ft/s);

Total velocity (ft/s);

Vertical acceleration (G);

Total acceleration (G);

Position East of launch (ft);

Position North of launch (ft);

Latitude;

Longitude;

Roll rate (r/s);

Pitch rate (r/s);

Yaw rate (r/s);

Vertical orientation (degree);

Lateral Orientation (degree);

Stability margin calibers;
